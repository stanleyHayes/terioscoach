## NURSE COACHING AGREEMENT

This Nurse Coaching Agreement (this “Agreement”) is between TERIOS WELLNESS SPA (the “Nurse”) and the client whose name appears in the signature line (the “Client”). The Client and Nurse are referred to as the “Parties”, and each a (“Party”). This Agreement is Effective as of the date specified in that attached Statement of Work. The Parties agree as follows:

1. Nurse Coaching. The Nurse shall provide the Client nurse coaching, which will involve direct and personal conversations conducted via scheduled face-to-face, video, or phone appointments. This may include, but is not limited to, one or more of the following: creation/ development of personal, professional, emotional, spiritual, mental, physical, and lifestyle goals and to design and carry out a strategy/plan for achieving those goals; identifying and addressing specific personal struggles, professional issues, or general physiological conditions; value clarification, brainstorming, identifying plans of action, examining modes of operation in life, asking clarifying questions, and making empowering requests or suggestions for action (collectively, “Nurse Coaching”).

2. Client Obligations. Nurse Coaching is a collaborative process. For success, the Client acknowledges that they:

1. Must be punctual, present, and undistracted during the coaching sessions;

2. Must invest time and energy in the Nurse Coaching sessions;

3. Must independently complete reflections and assignments and provide their full attention and energy to these assignments, as they are a valuable part of the Nurse Coaching process; and

4. May choose to disclose details of their past or present psychological, psychiatric, and/or medical history or treatment.

3. Session Scheduling. The Nurse Coaching sessions will be scheduled in advance at mutually agreed upon dates and times. To cancel a scheduled Nurse Coaching session, the Client must provide the Nurse at least 48-hours’ written notice (via e-mail or text) in order to be eligible for a reschedule. Missed appointments or cancellations within the 48-hour window will result in forfeiture of the coaching session. A particular session can only be rescheduled once. Of course, life happens, and emergencies do come up. In these cases, exceptions may be considered at the Nurse’s discretion. The Nurse reserves the right to cancel and reschedule coaching sessions as needed and will provide as much notice to the Client as possible.

4. Fees and Expenses.

1. Fees. Client shall pay the Nurse the fees as provided herein and as set out in the attached “Statement of Work.” Unless otherwise provided, fees will be payable upon receipt of invoice and in no event more than three (3) days thereafter.

2. Late Payments. Payments more than three (3) days overdue late. All late payments shall bear simple interest at 1%. The Nurse may suspend the Nurse Coaching when a payment is late.

3. Refund Policy. All fees paid by Client to Nurse under the Agreement are non-refundable. The Client is responsible for communicating any dissatisfaction with Nurse Coaching with the Nurse as soon as possible. Prompt communication will allow the Nurse to restructure the Nurse Coaching strategy accordingly.

1. Term. This Agreement shall commence as of the date the Agreement has been signed by all parties, (the “Effective Date”) and shall continue for the Initial Term specified in the Statements of Work, unless sooner terminated pursuant to Section 9.2 – 9.4.

Notwithstanding any language above to the contrary, the Nurse may terminate this Agreement before the end of the Initial Term on written notice if either (a) the Client fails to pay or (b) the Nurse, in its sole discretion, feels the relationship will not lead to a positive outcome for the Client. 1. Danger. Disclosure is compelled or permitted by the fact that the Client are in such a mental or emotional condition as to be dangerous to the Client or the person or property of others, and the Nurse determines that disclosure is necessary to prevent the threatened danger.

2. Abuse. Disclosure is mandated by the child abuse/neglect reporting laws or the elder/dependent adult abuse reporting laws. If the Nurse has a reasonable suspicion that abuse or neglect has occurred, the Nurse must report.

3. Public Health. To prevent and control disease, including communicable disease, the Nurse may be obligated to share your PHI.

If a lawsuit is filed against the Nurse or other legal proceeding, the Nurse may be required to disclose PHI by court of law, arbitration panel, taxing authority, and as part of the Nurse’s defense in court, or if an arbitrator or arbitration panel compels disclosure when arbitration is lawfully requested.
